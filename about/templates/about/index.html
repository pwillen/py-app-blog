About

I'm Preston. though online you'll usually find me as R4-D-17.

It took me a while to recognize it, but I'm an artist of sorts. Not necessarily in the traditional sense, and certainly not someone who has everything figured out. Somewhere along the way, between school, pursuing a STEM career, and settling into the daily 9-to-5 grind, I lost touch with that part of myself.

Purple Static is one small step toward getting it back.

A Creative in Progress

Growing up, I spent a lot of time drawing and gradually developed a skill that I was genuinely proud of. That changed when I went to college. My attention shifted toward academics and preparing for a career, and drawing slowly fell out of my life.

There's a part of me that wonders where I'd be if I'd kept at it. I sometimes feel like I left an ability unexplored, knowing that continued practice could have taken me somewhere interesting. But creativity doesn't always disappear just because you stop expressing it in one particular way.

Over time, I found myself appreciating artistic expression through other mediums. Movies, music, and eventually literature became new ways to explore the things that make art meaningful. I also realized how much I'd overlooked growing up, dismissing literature as something that had little relevance to the STEM career I was pursuing.

Now I'm making up for some of that lost ground. I'm exploring the works that interest me, learning to play electric guitar, experimenting with art, and finding new ways to create.

I don't have a grand artistic destination in mind. I'm more interested in seeing where curiosity takes me.

A Habit of Asking Why

There's a recurring theme throughout this site: investigation. I'm interested in understanding things beyond what they present on the surface. How they work, what makes them what they are, and how all their individual pieces come together to form something greater.

Why does this work the way it does? What makes it resonate with me? How can someone else experience the same thing so differently? What ideas, influences, and circumstances shaped it into what it is today?

I'm drawn to understanding the inner workings of things: the systems, relationships, and decisions that make an entity function as a whole. Whether it's the construction of an album, the visual language of a film, the architecture of a piece of software, or the techniques behind a work of art, I want to understand what makes it tick.

That curiosity is the foundation of Purple Static. It's what drives me to explore, learn, question, and ultimately find new ways to create.

Why R4-D-17?

R4-D-17 is my longtime gamer tag and general online persona. It comes from R4-P17, the astromech droid from Star Wars. I actually got the name wrong when I came up with it in early high school, and the accidental variation stuck.

There's some irony in that, because I don't particularly care for Star Wars these days. But after all this time, the name has become my own.

I've used other aliases over the years, too. Gleadr, for example, comes from the Inheritance Cycle (the Eragon series), another piece of fiction that left its mark on me.

Why Purple?

Purple has been my favorite color for as long as I can remember.

I've always been drawn to characters, creatures, and designs with some sort of purple presence. Beelzemon and Gengar are a couple of examples that come to mind. There's just something about the color that has always resonated with me.

So when it came time to give this little corner of the internet an identity, purple was never really up for debate.

And that's how we got Purple Static.
